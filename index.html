How it lives

Right where your eyes already are.

Privacy

Private by design.

An app that sees your music, messages and clipboard has to earn that. Here's how WinIsland does.

Your voice stays home

Speech is turned into text on your PC. Recordings are never kept, logged or sent.

Clipboard in memory only

Gone the moment WinIsland quits. Never written to disk, never synced.

Passwords skipped

Anything your password manager marks as private never enters the history, using the same markers as Windows.

Online only when you choose

Album-art lookups send just artist and title. Siro's brain sends only what your PC can't answer, with your own key. Both can be switched off.

Questions

Good questions.

When can I get WinIsland?

It's in the final stretch. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email the day it's out, with the early-bird price on Pro.

Should I download it here or from the Microsoft Store?

Either. It's the same app. The Store version installs in a click and keeps itself up to date.

Which versions of Windows does it run on?

Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) and Windows 11.

Which music apps work?

Any app that tells Windows what it's playing: Spotify, YouTube in Chrome or Edge, Amazon Music, Media Player and many more. When an app doesn't share artwork, WinIsland can look it up (or you can switch that off).

Is Siro always listening?

No. By default Siro only listens after you press the shortcut or tap the mic. “Hey Siro” is opt-in, and even then only the first two seconds after a pause are checked for the name, on your PC.

Does Siro's brain cost anything?

No. It uses Google Gemini with your own API key, free from Google AI Studio with no card needed. Only the requests your PC can't handle go to Google, and on Gemini's free tier Google may use them to improve its products. You can leave the brain off and still use every on-device command.

Which languages does Siro understand?

English and Hindi, and it works out which one you're speaking. With the brain on, Hinglish requests work too.

Will it get in the way of games or films?

No. It hides itself whenever a fullscreen app or game is in front, clicks pass straight through everywhere except the pill, and clicking the pill never steals focus. Siro's speech model is only loaded while you use it and unloads after ten minutes.

I have more than one monitor.

Pick which display the island sits on in Settings, and how far from the top edge.

Is this made by Apple or Microsoft?

Neither. WinIsland is an independent app, inspired by the island on phones and built natively for Windows.
